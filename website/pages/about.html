Our Heritage & Mission

Pure & Healthy Food for Every Family

THULIRVA is a natural dairy and agro brand born out of Kanchipuram, committed to bringing unadulterated, wholesome milk and farm goods directly from our pasture to your dining table.

Our cattle graze freely on organic pastures in Thimmarajampettai. We believe in traditional methods, cruelty-free dairying, and zero chemical preservatives — delivering only nature's purest nutrition.

Every morning, fresh milk is collected at dawn, chilled immediately to 4°C, and dispatched to your home before 7:00 AM.

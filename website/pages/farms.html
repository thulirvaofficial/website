Our Farm Standards

Sustainable & Scientific Agriculture

Every step of our process honors nature, animal welfare, and nutritional potency.

🌱

100% Organic Fodder

We grow maize, Napier grass, and lucerne using organic farm compost. No chemical fertilizers or toxic runoff.

🐄

Cruelty-Free Milking

Calves feed first. Milking is conducted in stress-free, automated parlours without human touch or oxytocin injections.

❄️

Instant 4°C Chilling

Fresh milk is immediately brought down to 4°C within 45 minutes to lock in vitamins and prevent any bacterial growth.

☀️

100% Solar-Powered

Our processing units and cold storage run entirely on renewable rooftop solar energy, preserving our ecological footprint.

🧪

Daily 32-Point Testing

Every single batch undergoes testing for adulterants, antibiotics, heavy metals, aflatoxins, and bacteria before packing.

💧

Rainwater Harvesting

Our farm collects millions of litres of monsoon water every year, replenishing local aquifers and groundwater tables.
